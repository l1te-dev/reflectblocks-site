Reflect Blocks — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Reflect Blocks is a single-player puzzle game. We do not run accounts or servers, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

Game data. Your scores, stats, settings and saved games are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

Daily leaderboard. If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your daily puzzle score is posted to a Game Center leaderboard run by Apple. Other players see you under a random name made up by the game (for example "Teal Fox 417"), never your Game Center name. Game Center is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can play without Game Center.

Ads. The game shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID, if you allow tracking), IP address (for approximate location), ad interaction and diagnostic data, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. If you decline tracking in the iOS prompt, you still see ads, but they are not personalized by cross-app tracking. In regions that require it, you will see a consent message from Google, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy options. See Google's ad policy.

Purchases. The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple. We receive no payment information.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices. You can turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Deleting the app deletes your saved games and stats.
