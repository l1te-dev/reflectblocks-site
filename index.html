Reflect Blocks — Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email cadpack.support@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How to play: drag pieces onto the 8×8 board. Every piece is mirrored across the glowing line, and the line moves with each new set of pieces. Fill rows or columns to clear them. Relax mode has no mirror.

Restore a purchase: Settings (gear icon) → Restore purchase.

Daily leaderboard: sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings. It resets every day at 00:00 UTC, when the new daily puzzle starts.

Ad privacy choices: Settings → Ad privacy options (where available), or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
